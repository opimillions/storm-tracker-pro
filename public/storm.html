About this data

Hail sizes are radar estimates from NOAA's Multi-Radar Multi-Sensor system. They show where large hail was likely, not damage at a given roof. Ground reports are preliminary spotter, public and law-enforcement reports compiled by NOAA's Storm Prediction Center.
