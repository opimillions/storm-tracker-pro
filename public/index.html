Hail size scale

What hail size means for a roof

Drawn to relative size. Every report and storm map uses the same plain-language scale, so homeowners, buyers and clients get it at a glance.

Minor

0.75–0.99″ · Penny

Cosmetic damage to soft metals and screens. Roof damage possible on older roofs.

Moderate

1.00–1.24″ · Quarter

Damage likely on aging asphalt shingles; dents to gutters, vents and screens.

Severe

1.25–1.74″ · Half dollar

Shingle bruising and granule loss likely, especially on older roofs.

Major

1.75–2.49″ · Golf ball

Widespread shingle damage, dented siding, gutters and vehicles.

Catastrophic

2.50″+ · Tennis ball

Roof punctures, broken windows. Full roof replacement is common.

Typical outcomes. Roof age, material and wind all change what a given storm does.

Straight answers

What contractors and adjusters ask us first

Will a carrier accept a radar report?

Radar doesn't prove damage, and no report can promise what a carrier will do. What you get is a dated, sourced storm history from NOAA radar and National Weather Service records to support the inspection and the claim file. When a claim is disputed, you can order a meteorologist-reviewed report from any property report.

I already pay for a hail service. Why switch?

Most charge by the address. StormCheck Pro is unlimited on every plan, and it adds swath lead lists, storm playback, portfolio alerts and CRM sync on top of the report.

How accurate is the hail size?

Sizes come from NOAA MRMS radar on a 1 km grid, estimated at the property itself instead of the nearest report miles away. Roof age, material and wind still matter, so treat it as where to look, then inspect.

Where does the storm data come from?

Three NOAA sources: MRMS radar estimates of maximum hail size on a 1 km grid, the National Weather Service's official Storm Events Database, and Storm Prediction Center spotter reports for the most recent months.

What does "Verified" mean?

Radar estimated hail of 0.75" or larger at the property, and at least one ground hail report was filed within 3 miles on the same day. Two independent sources agreeing is much stronger evidence than either alone.

How far back does the history go?

Every report covers the last 5 years of storms: radar hail estimates, official National Weather Service records and spotter reports.

Will my crew actually use it?

It works in the CRM you already run. JobNimbus and AccuLynx connect directly: your customers or jobs load in automatically, and when a property is hit we add a note to that customer or job. ClaimWizard, HubSpot, SPOTIO and other CRMs connect through Zapier or Make. The Team plan covers up to 5 people.

Where does it work?

Everywhere in the United States. Radar hail estimates cover the lower 48 states; storm records cover all states.

What do I get for free?

One full property report with the map, storm dates, verification and impact score. No credit card. Free hail alerts by ZIP code are free forever.

Can I use it before buying or listing a property?

Yes. The Storm Disclosure report is a one-page storm history built for listings, buyers and due diligence.

Can I put my logo on reports?

Pro adds your company name, Team adds your logo, and Enterprise is fully white-labeled with your colors.
